## 💡 Literature Review Summary & Research Gap Analysis

When writing your **Chapter 2 (Literature Review)** or paper introduction, use this synthesized narrative:

### 1. The Actuarial Problem
Historically, insurance companies relied upon **Generalized Linear Models (GLMs)** with log or gamma link functions. While GLMs provide direct interpretability, they struggle with high-order non-linear feature interactions (such as the exponential surge in medical charges when an applicant is **both** a smoker and has a BMI $> 30$).

### 2. The Machine Learning Evolution
Recent studies (Kamakshi 2022, ScienceDirect 2024, PMC 2022) demonstrate that ensemble methods—specifically **Random Forest**, **Gradient Boosting**, **XGBoost**, **LightGBM**, and **CatBoost**—consistently outperform regularized linear models (Ridge/Lasso) by achieving higher coefficients of determination ($R^2 > 0.84$) and substantially lower Mean Absolute Error (MAE $< \$2,500$).

### 3. The Research Gap: The Black-Box Dilemma
Despite high accuracy, ensemble models are frequently rejected in healthcare policy due to their opacity. Actuaries and regulatory bodies demand justifications for individual premium pricing. 

### 4. Our Contribution
Our project addresses this exact gap by combining:
1. An empirical 8-model competitive benchmark under strict leakage-free preprocessing.
2. **Shapley Additive Explanations (SHAP)** that decompose individual applicant predictions into transparent, additive dollar contributions ($+\$X$ or $-\$Y$).
3. An interactive Streamlit deployment making explainable regression actionable for non-technical stakeholders.
